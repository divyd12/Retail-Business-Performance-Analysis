# 🛒 Retail Business Performance & Profitability Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Retail Business Performance & Profitability Analysis** project is an end-to-end data analytics project focused on analyzing retail transactional data to understand overall business performance, sales trends, profitability, product performance, customer segments, and regional performance.

The main purpose of this project is to transform raw retail data into meaningful business insights using a complete data analytics workflow.

The project uses:

- 🐍 Python for data cleaning and exploratory data analysis
- 🗃️ MySQL and SQL for querying and business analysis
- 📊 Tableau for interactive data visualization and dashboard creation

The analysis helps identify high-performing and low-performing areas of the business and supports data-driven decision-making.

# 🎯 Project Objectives

The major objectives of this project are:

- To analyze overall retail business performance.
- To calculate important business KPIs such as sales, profit, orders, customers, and profit margin.
- To identify high-performing and low-performing product categories.
- To analyze category and sub-category profitability.
- To identify products with high sales and products generating losses.
- To analyze sales and profit across different geographical regions.
- To compare the performance of different customer segments.
- To study sales and profit trends over time.
- To identify seasonal patterns in retail performance.
- To perform SQL-based business analysis using MySQL.
- To create interactive visualizations using Tableau.
- To provide meaningful business insights and recommendations.
